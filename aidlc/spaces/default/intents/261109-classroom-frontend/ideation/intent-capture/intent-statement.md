# Intent Statement

## Problem Statement

This is the frontend for a classroom management system. The owner is an English teacher who finds tracking student attendance in Excel too much work and wants that tracking automated. [Q1] [Q2] [desc]

## Target Customer

The app is for that teacher alone. They are the only stakeholder and the only person who will use it for now. Later it might extend to other people. [Q2] [Q5]

## Success Metrics

- Most of the main classroom workflow can be finished in the app, even though the backend is not fully finished. [Q3]
- The screens look good from the start, with a consistent, documented design and color palette. [Q3] [Q11]
- Attendance can be tracked, and finance can be managed, for students in different classes. [Q9] [Q11]
- Most of the finished backend APIs are available in the frontend. [Q9]
- Each attendance record has a short note where the teacher jots what the student learnt. Learning is not a separate tracker. [Q10] [Q11]

## Initiative Trigger

This is mainly a personal project and a trial of the development process. The owner wants it done and working, and has been putting it off because they do not want to code the frontend. [Q4] [Q11]

## Initial Scope Signal

Workflow-selected scope: express. [scope]

This version's features are the ones already documented in openapi.yml. [Q8] [Q9] [desc]

Later, and only when the owner asks, on the backend: generate Google Meet classroom links, send them to students, and take attendance when they join. [Q8]

Later, and not built yet: an end-of-month email listing each student's tuition. [Q8] [Q9]

The first version stops when the owner is happy with attendance, finance across classes, and most of the finished backend APIs on the frontend. [Q9]

## Assumptions & Open Questions

None.
